# Dark Psychology vs. Healthy Boundaries: How to Spot Emotional Manipulation

## Description
Have you ever raised a concern and ended up defending yourself for having one? This Why We Become episode explores the difference between ordinary relationship conflict and repeated patterns of emotional manipulation—including gaslighting, love-bombing, controlling behavior, and the misuse of psychological labels.

Learn what healthy boundaries can look like, how accountability differs from recurring promises, and why you can care about someone without giving up your own choices or sense of self.

This video is educational, not a diagnosis or individualized mental-health advice. A disagreement alone does not prove manipulation. If you feel threatened or unsafe, prioritize safety and seek appropriate trusted or professional support before taking steps that may increase risk.

Think deeper. Live better. Become more.
